# RENM — Organiser-control discovery findings

Status: strategy evidence and manual-validation brief. Not an approved product build.

## Observation

Preparing organiser outreach from outbound-click evidence exposed recurring fragmentation across event identity, ownership and delivery roles, entry state, source freshness, attribution and appropriate contact routes. Publicly resolving one event may require distinguishing its recurring series and dated occurrence, organiser or owner, host club, race director, registration provider, timer and results operator.

## Bounded hypothesis

RENM may provide value as an organiser-controlled canonical public portfolio with authorised change distribution, conflict monitoring and attributable demand. It would connect, not replace, booking and results systems. Organisers without a durable website may value a stable RENM-hosted public record.

The rejected broad formulation is universal coverage of all races, clubs, platforms and results with guaranteed automatic syndication. Each external destination has separate incentives, authority, access and update mechanisms.

## Manual validation before software

Use organisers represented in audited outbound traffic. For each suitable contact:

1. Resolve roles and outreach eligibility.
2. Reconstruct a read-only portfolio and public-representation map.
3. Show conflicts, stale records and conservative outbound evidence.
4. Ask the organiser to correct or claim the portfolio.
5. Record which destinations matter and how each can be updated.
6. Ask for a second real update without repeated chasing.
7. Seek an authorised attribution or distribution pilot.
8. Record concrete willingness to maintain, introduce a partner or pay separately from verbal enthusiasm.

## Initial decision signals

Continue if the completed-conversation cohort produces approximately five claimed/corrected portfolios, three second updates, two authorised attribution/distribution tests and one concrete willingness-to-pay signal. Adjust interpretation for cohort size and organiser type.

Narrow if one organiser segment or one downstream destination clearly dominates demand.

Park if organisers regard existing booking pages as sufficient, do not correct meaningful errors, do not return, will not authorise a low-effort test and attach no commercial value to demand or distribution.
