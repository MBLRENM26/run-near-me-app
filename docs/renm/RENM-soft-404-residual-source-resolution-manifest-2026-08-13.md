## Classification rules used

- **ENRICH** — identity is resolved and the source supports existing public fields such as date, venue, race options, terrain/profile, fees, entry URL, organiser URL and governance.
- **DUPLICATE CHECK** — inspect live rows and aliases before choosing a canonical record. The suffix alone is not enough to merge or redirect.
- **VERIFY DATE** — keep out of the recrawl batch until the organiser or its registration provider confirms the exact occurrence.
- **MERGE OCCURRENCE** — one event occurrence with multiple race/distance options; preserve race-level facts without creating competing occurrence pages.
- **REDIRECTED ALIAS** — do not enrich the old URL; verify the redirect and sitemap/internal-link cleanup.
- **REMOVE FROM UK PUBLIC INVENTORY** — retain evidence privately if useful, but exclude from UK discovery, sitemap and indexability under the current lifecycle contract.
